Join the worship team

We are after the Lord. His presence, personally, before any of the music.

Wanting to be on this team is good. What we’re asking is bigger: come to lead people into what you’ve found in Him, not just to play or sing. Psalm 34:8

Before you are a musician here, you are someone who knows Him. That order never changes. We want the gift He gave you. It just comes second.

Start here

Before you join, we ask for a month with us. Four Sundays, two times in the prayer room, one rehearsal. You cannot fail any of it. It is just time, so He can meet you and so we can get to know you.

  1. 4

    Sundays at Modern Worship.

    Come to the 9:00 or the 11:00 for a month, so you see what we are actually leading people into.

  2. 2

    Times in the prayer room.

    Mondays 6–8 AM, Tuesdays and Thursdays 9–12, Wednesdays 5–8 PM. One of those fits around school or work. Just sit with Him. Nothing to perform, nothing to produce.

  3. 1

    Rehearsal, sat in on.

    Sundays at 7:00 AM, so you can see how we work together before you are in it.

Once those are done, tell me and we will talk.

What we look for

  • Sensitivity before skill.

    Knowing when to play and when to leave space. Following where He is leading, not just the chart in front of you.

  • Teachable.

    You can take a note without it wrecking you.

  • Still growing is fine.

    We would rather grow with you than hire someone who is already done.
