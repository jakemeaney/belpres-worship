Expectations

Holiness first

Be holy, because I am holy.

1 Peter 1:15-16

Everything below comes from that. This is what holiness looks like on a Tuesday, and at 7:00 on a Sunday morning, for people who lead a room to a holy God. Most of it is worked out in prayer long before it is worked out on a platform. And it is weightier than playing: Scripture says those who teach are judged more strictly, and leading a room in worship teaches them who God is whether or not you say a word. James 3:1 We are pursuing it together, and Scripture is plain that without it no one sees the Lord. Hebrews 12:14

Read who we are for why that order matters.

What we are after

A life laid down.

Looking at Him changes people. All of us, with unveiled faces, are being changed from one degree of glory to the next, which means none of us stays as we are. 2 Corinthians 3:18

And we are not being made into copies of each other. Different gifts, the same Spirit. Different kinds of service, the same Lord. 1 Corinthians 12:4-6

What He asks of you comes in your own voice, on your own schedule, out of your own history. The surrender is the same for every one of us. What it looks like is yours. So the question stays simple: have you handed your life over?

If you are new to this and do not understand it yet, that is a fine place to start. Nobody here arrived understanding it.

The rest of what we ask

  • Be with Him first.

    Paul says pray without ceasing. Not a thing you finish before the downbeat. You stay there the whole time.

    1 Thessalonians 5:17

  • Hand on what you know.

    Been here a while? When you make a decision, say why you made it, so somebody learns to hear it too.

    2 Timothy 2:2

  • Ask, and say what you hear.

    New here? Ask why we did something, then tell us what you notice. The Spirit gave you something we need.

    1 Corinthians 12:7

  • Decide together.

    Make your case, hear theirs out, then land on one call and get behind it.

    Philippians 2:3-4

  • Back each other up.

    Talk about someone the same way when the mics are off as you would with them on.

    Ephesians 4:29

  • Every task is worship.

    Coiling a cable counts the same as the song. Do all of it for the glory of God.

    1 Corinthians 10:31

Not the platform, not the set. Him. He was never a means to a good service. He is the whole point. And He settled how He feels about you at the cross, while you were still a long way off. That was true before you played a note, and it holds whatever kind of Sunday you have. Romans 5:8

Who decides what

So nobody has to guess, and so disagreement has somewhere to land.

  • Songs and keys

    MineBring me songs any time through Ask. I make the call, and I will tell you why.

  • Arrangements

    Ours, in rehearsalParts, dynamics, who takes what. Say it before we land it, then get behind what we land on.

  • In the moment

    Whoever is leadingIf we go somewhere the plan did not, follow the person up front. Sort out the disagreement afterward.

  • Scheduling

    Answer your scheduleAccept or decline in Planning Center within the week, and put your blockouts in before the schedule is built. A blank is the one answer that leaves everyone stuck. Let your yes be yes and your no be no.

  • Slides

    Serve on slidesAt least three Sundays a year. The words on the screen are how most of the room worships, and running them teaches you the service from the other side.

Paul told a church to do everything decently and in order, because God is a God of peace and not confusion. Same for us. 1 Corinthians 14:33 · 14:40

When something is off

Whoever rebukes a person will afterward find more favor than one who flatters with the tongue.

Proverbs 28:23

Clear is kind. Saying nothing and hoping it improves is neither, and Scripture treats flattery as the opposite of love. So when something here is off, we say it. Plainly, early, and to the person it concerns. Jesus gave us the order.

  1. 01

    Go to them yourself, first.

    Alone, and directly. Talking to a third person about it before you talk to them is the thing Scripture is ruling out.

  2. 02

    If that does not land, take one or two with you.

    People who will hear both sides, and who care about the person more than the point.

  3. 03

    If it still does not land, bring it to me.

    Then we decide together what changes, and I will say it to you straight.

Matthew 18:15-17

The aim is always to restore, in a spirit of gentleness, watching ourselves while we do it. Correction here is for keeping people, not for winning. Galatians 6:1

This runs both directions. If I have missed something, or handled something badly, come to me first. Same order, same day, same grace. I would rather be told than protected from it.
