Who we are

Mission

To make a resting place for God among us, and to lead His people into it.

Where a resting place comes from

God has always wanted to live with His people. Not visit them. Live with them. You can follow that one thing straight through the Bible, and the only part that ever changes is where He does it.

  1. Eden

    They heard the sound of the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool of the day.

    Genesis 3:8

  2. A tent

    Out in the desert God tells Moses to build Him a tent, so He can camp in the middle of the people. Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them.

    Exodus 25:8

  3. A temple

    Later the tent becomes a building in Jerusalem. God says of it, this is my resting place forever; here I will dwell, for I have desired it.

    Psalm 132:13-14

  4. David wanted to build it

    He called it a house of rest for God, and he never got to build it. God let his son do it instead.

    1 Chronicles 28:2

  5. Christ

    God moves in Himself. John says the Word became flesh and lived among us, and the word he uses is the same one for that tent in the desert. God pitched His tent with us.

    John 1:14

  6. Us

    Paul says you are God’s temple and His Spirit lives in you. No building. People. And we are being built together into a place for God to live.

    1 Corinthians 3:16 · Ephesians 2:22

  7. Forever

    The last book ends where it started. God living with people, and this time it does not end.

    Revelation 21:3

There is no one verse that says this is your job. It is the whole story, from the first book to the last. And right now the place He lives is people. That is what a rehearsal and a prayer room and a Sunday morning are actually for.

Vision

A team who worships Him through the week, leading a church that knows who He is.

Two halves. Us: people who have already been with Him before Sunday, so what happens on the platform is overflow and not a performance we work up that morning.

Them: a church that knows God better every year because of what we sing. Not just moved. Knowing Him.

What comes first

Whatever is going on with a leader in private eventually shows up in what they lead in public. So we put these in order.

  1. 01

    Your own worship.

    You cannot take people somewhere you have not been. Jesus got up before light and went out to a quiet place to be with His Father, and then did only what He saw the Father doing. That is what we are copying, on the platform and off it.

    Mark 1:35 · John 5:19

  2. 02

    Character, then craft.

    Skill matters and we work at it. It is still in service of leading people. Jesus said apart from Him we can do nothing, and He meant nothing.

    John 15:4-5

  3. 03

    Years, not one Sunday.

    We are shaping what this church believes about God over years. None of us has arrived. Paul said he was still pressing on, so we are in good company.

    Philippians 3:12

We would rather have a team of people who are genuinely being changed by God and still growing musically, than a polished team disconnected from the God they are leading people to. What matters most is whether we actually know Him in private, where nobody is watching. Being close to Him is the one thing worth asking for. Psalm 27:4

What we are guarding against

Two ways to get this wrong. We are honest that both are temptations for us.

Worship reduced to feeling.

Songs about being met, being loved, being comforted, with nothing about who God actually is. All of that is true and we need it. But if that is all we ever sing, people leave knowing they are loved and knowing nothing else about Him.

Worship reduced to information.

Every word carefully checked, and none of it landing anywhere. We say true things about God and nobody meets Him. The room gets it right and feels nothing.

We are after worship where God is the subject, and where the room gets real space to answer Him.
